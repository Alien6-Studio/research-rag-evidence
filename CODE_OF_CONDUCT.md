# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss concrete behavior, methods, and evidence.
Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of private
information. Welcome corrections and contributors with different experience levels.

These expectations apply to this project's issues, discussions, and pull requests.
Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@alien6.com](mailto:contact@alien6.com).
The maintainer reviews reports confidentially and may moderate content or restrict
participation proportionately. Concerns involving the maintainer may be escalated
to Alien6 Studio through the same organizational contact.
